# BEES Health product direction

## Core promise
Patients should leave a consultation understanding what happened and what to do next. BEES Health turns a consented recording into an accessible summary, but makes clinician confirmation a technical release gate rather than a disclaimer.

## Primary journey
1. Bee/Bee Eclipse shows purpose, recording state, retention and sharing destination.
2. Patient and clinician separately consent; either can stop.
3. Transcription and grounded summarisation produce a draft with source timestamps.
4. Clinician edits/confirms medications, actions and red flags.
5. Patient receives it in Bee or through a short-lived recipient-bound link.
6. Confirmed instructions can become reminders only after patient approval.

## Non-goals
Autonomous diagnosis, triage, medical-image interpretation in the MVP, hidden recording, unreviewed release, PHI in notifications, or representing the product as a cleared device.
